<h2>What is Dry Eye?</h2>

<p>Dry eye is a <a href="https://www.aoa.org/healthy-eyes/eye-and-vision-conditions/dry-eye?sso=y" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">common eye condition</a> that occurs when your eyes don’t have enough tears to stay hydrated. This can happen in <a href="https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6202299/#:~:text=There%20are%20two%20main%20subtypes,disease%20%E2%80%93%20aqueous%20deficiency%20and%20evaporative." target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">2 ways</a>:</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Aqueous deficiency dry eye</strong> is when your lacrimal glands don&#8217;t produce enough tears.</li>

<li><strong>Evaporative dry eye</strong> is usually caused by meibomian gland dysfunction (MGD), where the eyelid glands don&#8217;t secrete enough oil into the tears. This <a href="https://www.aao.org/eye-health/tips-prevention/facts-about-tears" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">oil layer</a> is crucial for preventing tear film evaporation.</li>
</ul>

<p>Whatever type you’re dealing with, dry eye can feel like having sandpaper for eyelids, and it comes with a variety of charming symptoms that everyday life a little tougher. Here are some of the greatest hits of dry eye symptoms:</p>

<ul>
<li>Redness</li>

<li>Sensitivity to light</li>

<li>Blurred vision</li>

<li>Eye fatigue</li>

<li>A stinging or burning sensation</li>

<li>Difficulty wearing contact lenses</li>
</ul>

<p>Sometimes though, there might be no symptoms at all. Because dry eye can be asymptomatic, it’s best to visit your eye doctor for regular eye exams to catch eye health issues like dry eye.</p>

<p>If you’re searching for relief from dry eye, IPL (intense pulsed light) therapy and RF (radio frequency) treatment offer promising solutions.</p>

<h2>IPL Therapy for Dry Eye</h2>

<p><a href="https://www.healthline.com/health/chronic-dry-eye/intense-pulsed-light-therapy-for-chronic-dry-eye#what-is-ipl-treatment" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">IPL therapy</a> uses specific wavelengths of light to target and reduce the inflammation that contributes to dry eye. By emitting pulses of light, the treatment helps melt blockages that generate inflammation, allowing the tear glands to function better.</p>

<p>This treatment can address the root cause of most dry eye cases, <a href="https://eyewiki.aao.org/Intense_Pulsed_Light_(IPL)_Therapy#Meibomian_gland_dysfunction" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">meibomian gland dysfunction</a> (MGD), which prevents the eye from quality tears. These low-quality tears can evaporate too fast, leaving your eyes without soothing hydration.</p>

<p>Interestingly, the light from IPL <a href="https://www.healthline.com/health/ipl-treatment" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">can also have aesthetic benefits</a>, reducing the appearance of sun damage, age spots, and rosacea.</p>

<h2>RF Treatment for Dry Eye</h2>

<p>RF treatment uses radiofrequency energy to <a href="https://www.healthline.com/health/eye-health/radiofrequency-treatment-for-dry-eye#how-can-rf-treat-dry-eye" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">stimulate collagen production</a> and melt thick oil stuck in your meibomian glands. This technique involves delivering controlled heat to the targeted area, which can enhance oil secretion and stabilize the tear film, alleviating dry eye symptoms.</p>

<p>Much like IPL, RF technology has aesthetic benefits. It can encourage the natural healing processes, tightening skin and smoothing fine lines.</p>
