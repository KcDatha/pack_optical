- Eyebrow: Dry Eye Clinic · Center of Excellence

- What: Tears are made of oil, water and mucus. ### ipl -> /services/ipl-treatment/

- Intro: The next generation of dry eye relief: InMode OptimaMAX combines IPL + RF to calm inflammation and restart oil flow — with a photofacial bonus for the skin around your eyes.

- Accordion "IPL vs drops": Drops replace tears for a few minutes. IPL treats why the tears fail — the inflamed, blocked glands — so relief compounds across sessions.
